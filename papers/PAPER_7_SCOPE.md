## 1. Framing (locked)

> "Out of respect for the IDP term's protections and definitional discipline (UN Guiding Principles 1998, Kampala Convention, IDMC/IOM tracking infrastructure), we coin **SDP (Structurally Displaced Persons)** as a parallel analytical concept for populations forced into displacement by institutional / policy mechanisms. Whether SDP and IDP describe the same mechanism, overlapping mechanisms, or distinct mechanisms is treated as an empirical question this paper takes up."

**Key framing choices** (locked, not negotiable downstream):

- SDP is a **coined parallel term**, not an assertion that US homelessness IS IDP
- SDP-IDP equivalence is an **empirical question**, not a foregone conclusion
- The framework is **correlational + revealed-preference**, never causal
- The political claim (state spending priorities) is **separable** from the empirical framework

## 2. Mechanism claim

**Hypothesis (H1):** SDP outcomes are produced by institutional / policy mechanisms ("structural force"). The proximate force in the US case is **state revealed-preference for war-making over welfare-making spending**, operating at two scales:

- **First-order**: total allocation between military and welfare-state architecture
- **Second-order**: within-welfare allocation priorities (e.g., housing-specific vs other welfare components)

**Hypothesis (H2):** The channel-orthogonality + residue-class methodology (Papers 2 + 4 + 6 substrate) applies to SDP as it does to IDP. SDP states cluster into "homelessness regimes" parallel to IDP residue-classes.

**Hypothesis (H3 — the test of SDP-IDP equivalence):** If SDP and IDP describe the same mechanism, channel structures should be similar across the two; residue-class structures should map. If distinct, the methodology applies but the substantive content differs.

## 3. Operationalized definitions (locked)

### "Voluntary" reallocation
**Voluntary = not externally imposed by occupying power, treaty constraint, or military defeat.**

- Captures: Costa Rica 1948 ✓, Mauritius 1968 ✓, peace dividend 1990s ✓, Finland Housing First 2008+ ✓
- Excludes: Japan post-1947 (Article 9), Panama 1990 (post-US invasion), Germany pre-1955

Strategic motivation doesn't disqualify; external coercion does.

### "Force" criterion for SDP
**State revealed-preference operating through institutional / policy mechanism**, where:
1. Alternative allocations existed (counterfactual existence — peer countries demonstrate)
2. Allocation pattern is sustained across administrations (revealed preference, not single decision)
3. Pattern is policy-produced, not natural law

NOT claiming: deterministic causation, partisan blame, single-policy attribution.

## 5. Anchor cases

### Primary anchor: US homelessness
- HUD AHAR PIT counts 2007-2024 (~653K 2023; ~196/100K)
- Eviction Lab county-level eviction filings 2000-2024
- ACS housing cost-burdened share
- US state-year panel as primary unit of analysis

### Comparison cases

| Case | Reallocation type | Date | Outcome focus | Data status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Costa Rica** | First-order: military → education + healthcare | 1948 | Life expectancy, education attainment, democracy continuity (NOT homelessness — methodologically incommensurable) | Public OECD + World Bank |
| **Mauritius** | First-order: never built military → industrial + welfare | 1968 | GDP per capita, life expectancy, education | World Bank |
| **Peace dividend wave** | First-order: military reduction (1990-1999); allocation varied across countries; confounded by Maastricht 1992 fiscal discipline | 1990-1999 | Post-2007 standardized OECD homelessness data + welfare-state intensity | SIPRI + OECD SOCX |
| **Finland Housing First** | Second-order: within-welfare → housing-specific | 2008+ | Homelessness consistently declining (only EU country with sustained decline) | ARA / Finnish Government reports + EU Housing First Hub |

### Why this comparison set works
- Costa Rica: deep-case voluntary first-order reallocation, sustained 75+ years
- Mauritius: independence-era first-order choice, sustained
- Peace dividend: wave-level natural-experiment with explicit Maastricht confounder
- Finland: second-order reallocation with the cleanest single-outcome story (homelessness decline)
- Methodological transparency on data quality + comparability
