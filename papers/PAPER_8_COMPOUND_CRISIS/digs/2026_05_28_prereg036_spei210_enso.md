# Paper 8 — PRE_REG_036: SPEI v2.10 meteorological-drought ENSO test (P8-F)

**Fired**: 2026-05-28
**Pre-reg**: PRE_REG_036 (locked before data; SPEI accessed via Google Earth Engine `CSIC/SPEI/2_10`)
**Data**: SPEIbase v2.10 (1901-2023, CRU TS 4.08) Dec 12-month + 3-month area-means over ETH/SOM/BRA boxes (GEE server-side reduce, 1950-2023) + NOAA ONI (OND).
**Status**: **Set A NOT SUPPORTED (F1); Set C F2 fired — the artifact hypothesis is REJECTED.** Two clean secondary findings (severity-dependence + Set B on OND band). One diagnostic limitation (box-mean dilution).

---

## Headline

**I built PRE_REG_036 to test whether PRE_REG_034 Set A's failure (Horn drought-DISPLACEMENT only 50% La Niña, F1) was a displacement-instrument artifact. The pre-committed expectation was that dense meteorological SPEI would show the ≥60% La Niña enrichment displacement counts missed. It does not.** Horn country-box SPEI-drought years are **50% La Niña (annual SPEI_12) / 43% (OND SPEI_03)** — essentially identical to the displacement-based 50%. So **the ~50% is real at the country-aggregate scale, NOT an instrument artifact** (F2). The single-year-La-Niña → Horn-drought mechanism is genuinely weak at this spatial scale; PRE_REG_034's walk-back stands firmer, now corroborated by the proper instrument.

Two findings survive clean: (1) the most **extreme** Horn droughts (SPEI ≤ −1.5) ARE 100% La Niña — La Niña drives the tail, not the bulk; (2) BRA Amazon OND-droughts are 0% La Niña / 100% El Niño — opposite the Horn, confirming the two-family opposite-signature result.

## Set A — Horn La Niña enrichment: NOT SUPPORTED (F1)

Locked primary: annual SPEI_12 (Dec), country-box mean, drought year = box-mean SPEI ≤ −1.0, full window 1950-2023, baseline La Niña 35.1%.

| band | Horn drought-years (box-mean ≤ −1.0) | La Niña % | enrichment |
|---|---|:---:|:---:|
| annual SPEI_12 | 1955, 1959, 1973, 1980, 1984, 2009 | **50.0%** (3LN/1EN/2neu of 6) | +14.9pp |
| OND SPEI_03 | 1955,1958,1969,1974,1975,1985,1986 | 42.9% (3LN/3EN of 7) | +7.8pp |

Predicted ≥60% AND enrichment ≥15pp. Observed 50% / +14.9pp — **F1 FIRED.** There is *modest* enrichment over baseline (La Niña years are ~1.4× over-represented), but it does not reach the pre-committed bar.

## Set C — instrument-artifact resolution: F2 (artifact REJECTED)

- Displacement-based (PRE_REG_034 Set A): Horn 50% La Niña.
- SPEI-based (this test): Horn 50% La Niña (annual) / 43% (OND).
- Pre-committed Set C: SPEI exceeds 50% by ≥15pp ⇒ displacement was the artifact. **Observed: SPEI ≈ displacement (Δ ≈ 0 to −7pp). F2 FIRED.**

**The ~50% Horn drought–La Niña association is a real feature, not a displacement-instrument artifact.** Switching from displacement counts (4 lagged years) to dense meteorological SPEI (74 contemporaneous years) did not change the answer. The single-year-ENSO mechanism is genuinely weak at country-aggregate scale.

## Severity-dependence (pre-committed sensitivity sweep) — clean secondary finding

| threshold | Horn drought-years | La Niña % |
|---|---|:---:|
| ≤ −0.8 (n=10) | …1954,1955,1958,1959,1973,1974,1980,1984,2002,2009 | 50% |
| ≤ −1.0 (n=6) | 1955,1959,1973,1980,1984,2009 | 50% |
| ≤ −1.5 (n=2, annual) | **1955, 1984** | **100%** |
| ≤ −1.5 (n=1, OND) | **1974** | **100%** |

**The most extreme Horn droughts are La-Niña-locked; moderate dry years are ENSO-mixed.** La Niña governs the severe tail (1955, 1974, 1984 are canonical La Niña Horn famines) but not the bulk of below-median years. This is the honest, non-trivial structure: ENSO is a tail-risk amplifier, not a year-to-year driver.

## Set B — Amazon opposite phase: SUPPORTED on the OND band

| band | BRA drought-years (≤ −1.0) | La Niña % | El Niño % |
|---|---|:---:|:---:|
| OND SPEI_03 | **1969, 2015** | **0%** | **100%** |
| annual SPEI_12 | 1983, 2015, 2016 | 66.7% (spurious) | 33.3% |

The **contemporaneous OND band cleanly shows BRA Amazon drought is El-Niño-aligned (0% La Niña), opposite the Horn** — the two-family opposite-signature finding (PRE_REG_034 Set C) survives at the meteorological level. The annual SPEI_12 band gives a **spurious 66.7% La Niña** because the 2015-16 El Niño Amazon drought lands in the 2016 annual index *after* ENSO flipped to weak La Niña in OND 2016 — the same year-labeling timing-smear that affected the displacement test. **Instrument-timing lesson: contemporaneous-season SPEI (SPEI_03-Dec) maps to ENSO correctly; annual SPEI_12-Dec smears it.**

## Diagnostic limitation — country-box MEAN dilutes spatially-concentrated drought

The 2020-2023 Horn drought — worst in 40 years, five consecutive failed seasons, textbook multi-year La Niña, >1M displaced — registers as **near-zero box-mean SPEI** (ETH_12 2022 = +0.05, SOM_12 2022 = −0.30) and never trips the −1.0 threshold. The drought was concentrated in the arid SE lowlands (Somali region, Borena, southern Somalia) and is washed out by averaging with the wet Ethiopian highlands inside the country box. Consequently the **2008-2023 window is degenerate** (only Horn "drought" = 2009, an El Niño year). This is a measurement pathology independent of the ENSO question (the metric cannot see a drought all sources agree happened), and it caps what the box-mean test can conclude about recent events.

**Motivated follow-up (NOT fired):** re-pull SPEI with a spatially-resolved drought metric — either a drought-epicenter sub-box (Horn ASAL lowlands) or **area-fraction-in-drought** (% of box area with SPEI ≤ −1) / a low areal percentile — to test whether the La Niña signal sharpens when the dilution is removed. To be locked as a separate pre-reg (PRE_REG_037) before firing, so it is diagnostic-driven, not result-driven.

## Net result + what it means for Paper 8

**PRE_REG_036 does not rescue the ENSO mechanism; it confirms the walk-back.** With the best available instrument (dense meteorological SPEI v2.10, 74 years, contemporaneous), the Horn country-box drought–La Niña association is ~50% — real but modest, and not an artifact of the earlier displacement data. ENSO acts as an **extreme-tail amplifier** (severe Horn droughts are La-Niña-locked) rather than a year-to-year coupling driver. The Amazon opposite-signature (El Niño) holds.

Updated Paper 8 mechanism statement (across PRE_REG_033/034/035/036): compound-crisis coupling is **rare (8%), structural not window-transient, predicted by chronic shock-overlap (not fragility), with family-specific climate signatures (Horn La-Niña-tail / Amazon El-Niño) — but NOT explained by single-year ENSO phase at country-aggregate scale.** The climate driver is a tail-risk modulator, not the coupling mechanism itself. Honest, refinement-rich; the spatial-resolution question is the live follow-up.
