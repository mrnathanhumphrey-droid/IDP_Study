## 5. Regime 6 event-latency (Phase 3 substantive refinement)

Phase 3 stability test (PRE_REG_014) revealed **4 of 6 Regime-6 members gained R6 only after 2007** via single major quake events:

| Country | 1980-2007 classification | 2008-2024 classification | Driving event |
|---|---|---|---|
| HTI | 3a (storm) | 6 (EQ) | 2010 Port-au-Prince Mw 7.0 |
| NPL | UNCLASSIFIED | 6 (EQ) | 2015 Gorkha Mw 7.8 |
| CHL | 4c (mixed) | 6 (EQ) | 2010 Maule Mw 8.8 + 2014 Iquique |
| ECU | 4a (flood-mixed) | 6 (EQ) | 2016 Pedernales Mw 7.8 |
| TUR | 6 (EQ) | 6 (EQ) | stable — multiple historic + 2023 |
| ITA | 6 (EQ) | 6 (EQ) | stable — L'Aquila + Irpinia 1980 |

**Refined Regime 6 definition (Phase 3 finding)**:

> Regime 6 status is the **joint product** of (geophysical exposure — subduction/collision zone tectonics) × (major-event occurrence within observation window).

A country's R6 classification is **window-sensitive**: "latent R6" (exposed but no major event in window) vs "activated R6" (event occurs, displacement registers, classification triggers).

**Latent R6 candidates** that could activate: IRN (Zagros + Alborz active), GRC (1981+1995 historical R6), NZL (Christchurch 2011), MEX/IDN (exposure exists but other channels dominate).

**Paper 2 implication**: regime classification must be reported with observation-window caveat. Storm and flood regimes more stable than R6 across decadal windows.

**Walk-backs**:
- H2 (state-capacity moderation): 0/3 contrasts meet threshold; ITA(97%) > NPL(30%) and JPN(38%) > HTI(31%) INVERTED. **Reporting heterogeneity dominates** state-capacity effect.

**New post-hoc hypothesis**: 3a >> 3b ratio reflects **mandatory-evacuation counting** in bimodal-mega-storm regimes vs **in-place-shelter** in perpetual-storm regimes. Needs forward pre-reg if formally claimed.
