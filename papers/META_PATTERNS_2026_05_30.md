# Meta-Patterns Across the IDP Program (2026-05-30)

**Purpose**: Cross-paper pattern-match — what recurs across all 8 papers that no single paper would surface alone? Reads from [SYNTHESIS_2026_05_29.md](SYNTHESIS_2026_05_29.md). This is an analytical synthesis-of-syntheses, not a manuscript draft.

---

## 0. Headline meta-claim (the unifying frame)

Across all 8 papers, the program is doing the **same epistemological move at the substrate level**:

> **The observed phenomenon is rarely the generative phenomenon.** The generative structure consistently lives **one resolution finer** than the observed unit (sub-national, period-specific, configuration-specific), **downstream of the apparent cause** (destination, mediator, consequence), and is **structural-chronic rather than acute-event**. The empirical breakthrough in each paper is *finding the right unit of analysis* — and it is consistently *not* the obvious one.

Six substantive cross-paper patterns flow from this, plus three methodological patterns about how the program produces refinements. The unifying frame is: **the IDP program is, recursively, an exercise in re-pivoting from observed to generative units.**

---

## 1. RIGHT-UNIT-OF-ANALYSIS — the substantive headline

In every paper, the empirical breakthrough is the identification of an analytical unit that is *not* the obvious one. The "obvious" unit (country, year, event, hazard) hides the structure; the "right" unit reveals it.

| paper | obvious unit | RIGHT unit found | what the right unit reveals |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 Exec aggrandizement | country-year V-Dem | success cases × failure cases (PATTERN_013 × PATTERN_027) | 4-lever blocking model; mechanism only legible with both sides |
| P2 Disaster regimes | country | regime (R1-R7) — country-period typology | 7 mechanism-distinct displacement modes; off-the-shelf country covariates can't reproduce |
| P3 Strife epicenter | regional aggregate | epicenter (MLI) + lag-diffused neighbours | 8-9 yr emergence lag from a single source |
| P4 Conflict typology | country | country-period type (A/B/C/D/E) | conflict-FORM > country identity (PRE_REG_021 F4 not fired; IRQ/NGA within-country type-shifts) |
| P5 Democratic resilience | country recovery trajectory | discriminating-lever configuration | civil-society + federalism discriminate (NOT courts/elections) |
| P6 Methodology | country-year displacement | residue-class typology | irreducible to WDI demographic (+1.28) or Polity fragility (−0.42); Stan ΔLOO +13.66 |
| P7 SDP / homelessness | state-level homelessness | metro/CoC + rent mediator | rent is the displacement funnel (R²=0.76); supply + demand both feed it through rent |
| **P8 compound-crisis coupling** | country-year national coupling | **destination hub × year** | 8 SOM cells = 56% burden; same-year ρ=+0.80 inside the hubs = the national coupling itself |

**The right unit is consistently** (a) **one resolution finer** than the obvious aggregate, AND (b) **a configuration / mediator / destination / type** rather than a raw entity. The right unit is *relational*, not nominal.

## 2. DOWNSTREAM MECHANISM — what looks upstream is actually downstream

A second meta-pattern repeats across half the papers: the program keeps finding that the **mechanism lives downstream of where naive intuition would place it**. The "obvious cause" is repeatedly revealed as either a consequence or a co-symptom of something further down the chain.

| paper | naive upstream candidate | actual downstream mechanism | resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 (PRE_REG_007) | emigration drives backsliding | emigration = late-stage CONSEQUENCE (VEN libdem collapse 1999-2008 precedes mass emigration 2015-2020) | v1 walked back, v2 supported |
| P7 (PRE_REG_031) | housing supply drives homelessness | rent (mediator) is the displacement funnel; supply + demand both feed it through rent (R²=0.76; indirect +0.18 + +0.56) | mediator analysis at n=302 CoCs |
| P8 (PRE_REG_036/037) | ENSO drives Horn coupling | climate is a weak tail-modulator; ENSO triangulated-null across 3 instruments | hazard-driver hypothesis rejected |
| P8 (PRE_REG_039) | local drought triggers conflict | within-cell drought→conflict NEGATIVE in coupling cases; positive only in SYR/AFG/IRQ (the literature's textbook cases — NOT the displacement-coupling ones) | Set C reversed |
| **P8 (PRE_REG_040/041)** | **coupling = same place gets both hazards** | **coupling = same destination receives both displaced flows in the same year (SOM dest ρ=+0.41, origin −0.10; 8 hubs = 56% burden; same-year ρ=+0.80)** | mechanism complete |

**The repeated finding**: the "compound crisis," the "supply crisis," the "emigration crisis" all live at the convergence point downstream of the obvious upstream cause, not where you'd naively look. Naively-upstream variables (supply, hazards, emigration) are co-symptoms or weak drivers; downstream variables (rent, destination, consequence) are mechanism-load-bearing.

A corollary: **the "Syria-2007-drought" / climate-conflict literature signal is real but specifically does not generalize to the displacement-coupling countries.** PRE_REG_039 found the local drought→conflict trigger in SYR/AFG/IRQ (the textbook protracted-war cases) and *not* in ETH/SOM/COD (the actual compound-coupling cases). That's a meta-finding about a literature: the canonical climate-conflict cases are not the canonical compound-displacement cases.

## 3. DISCRIMINATING FEATURES ≠ ONSET FEATURES — saturation/selection structure

Across multiple papers, the features that are PRESENT at onset (and that the literature traditionally tracks) are *different* from the features that DISCRIMINATE outcomes.

| paper | features present at onset | features that DISCRIMINATE the outcome |
|---|---|---|
| P1 capture | apex court, civil society, electoral cycle, federalism (all 4 levers typically discussed) | the 4-lever *configuration* (3+ present → blocked; ≤2 → succeeds) — the count + interaction matters, not any single one |
| P5 recovery | courts + elections + civil-society + federalism (all 4 levers at recovery-window start) | **civil-society + federalism** discriminate; courts/elections are near-universal at onset → not discriminating |
| P4 within-Type-C | multi-actor non-state insurgency (the defining feature) | within-C is heterogeneous; sub-typing candidate (the defining feature doesn't discriminate within-class) |
| P8 coupling | shock-overlap (chronic) + fragility (commonly assumed) | shock-overlap p=.001 IS discriminating; **fragility p=.125 is NOT** (PRE_REG_033) |
| P8 hazard coloc | drought-prone cells + conflict cells (the obvious co-location candidates) | non-coupling controls also have both — co-location at destinations (not hazards) discriminates |

**The recurring move**: stop tracking the feature that the literature has been tracking; track the feature that the literature has been *missing*. State-fragility doesn't predict compound coupling (shock-overlap does); courts/elections don't discriminate recovery (civil-society/federalism do); single levers don't predict capture (configurations do).

This is a methodologically important and underappreciated finding. The literature is dense at the onset-feature level and sparse at the discriminating-feature level, precisely because the former is what's measured and discussed.

## 4. SCALE-OF-RESOLUTION DETERMINES VISIBILITY

The choice of spatial / temporal / aggregation resolution determines whether the mechanism is visible. The same data, at different scales, gives different answers — and the country aggregate is usually the wrong scale.

| paper | aggregate-scale finding | finer-scale finding | what the scale change reveals |
|---|---|---|---|
| P2 (036) | country-box mean SPEI: SOM 2022 = −0.30 (invisible) | sub-national: 36.5% area in drought | the 2020-22 Horn catastrophe registers at sub-national but not country scale |
| P4 spatial conc. | type-mean conc. discriminates types | within-Type-C heterogeneity (P4-J/K) | sub-type structure exists inside Type C; aggregate type masks it |
| P8 (038) | national coupling correlation (033, lagged/structural) | district scale 0.5° (+0.30 mixed) → washes out at 2° | co-location is a 50-100km phenomenon, not coarser |
| P8 (041) | national year-coupling | within destination hubs, cell-year ρ=+0.80; non-hubs +0.17 ns | national coupling IS the aggregate signature of a few hubs |
| P1 (regional) | regional cluster conditioning | range conditioning (libdem level on continuous axis) | CAF inside Central Africa proved within-cluster — categorical regional cluster < continuous level |

**The pattern**: country/year/regional aggregates almost always under-represent the signal. The mechanism is **fine-scale + continuous**; the aggregate is **coarse + categorical**. Two corollaries:

- **Range > cluster** (continuous conditioning beats categorical cluster conditioning).
- **District > country, period > country-year, mediator > exposure** (one resolution finer always wins).

## 5. STRUCTURAL-CHRONIC > ACUTE-EVENT

Across papers, long-run structural / chronic features outperform short-run event-driven features in mechanism explanation.

| paper | acute-event candidate (rejected/weak) | structural-chronic mechanism (load-bearing) |
|---|---|---|
| P8 coupling drivers | single-year ENSO phase (drought, conflict, both) | chronic shock-overlap (PRE_REG_033 p=.001); structural — leave-window-out unchanged (035) |
| P8 vs fragility | acute fragility events | shock-overlap, NOT state-fragility (p=.125) |
| P1 backsliding speed | crises / triggers | federal-friction (structural slow-burn class); 4-lever configuration |
| P5 recovery | event-driven recoveries | structural plateau (BRA 2% / KOR 4%); vertical-tier capped |
| P4 DPF | event-driven displacement | type-structural DPF rank order (D < A < C < B < E) — a chronic property of conflict form |
| P2 climate attribution | single storm/heat-wave events | chronic Set C ACE × storm-IDP +0.552 (SST → ACE → displacement chain) |

**The recurring finding**: structural / chronic features explain the bulk of variance; acute events ride atop a structural baseline. Where literatures emphasize triggers, this program finds **substrates**.

## 6. DISTINCT ORIGINS + STRUCTURALLY-LINKED-DOWNSTREAM

A third structural pattern: phenomena that *look* like co-located or shared upstream actually have **distinct origins + linked-downstream structure**.

| paper | apparent "shared" upstream | actual distinct-origins + linked-downstream structure |
|---|---|---|
| **P8 coupling** | drought and conflict in the same places | distinct hazard origins (drought peripheries vs conflict zones) + shared displacement destinations (8 SOM hubs absorb both flows) |
| P3 strife | regional Sahel diffusion as a shared phenomenon | distinct origin (MLI epicenter) + structurally-linked lagged diffusion (BFA 8yr, NER 9yr) |
| P2 climate attribution | "climate drives displacement" | distinct climate origins (SST anomalies, ACE) + linked displacement-chain (SST → ACE → IDP) |
| P7 SDP framework | homelessness as singular issue | distinct supply + demand origins + shared rent funnel → same homelessness outcome |
| P1 backsliding | "the third wave of autocratization" as shared | distinct trajectories per case + Bukelization path as the shared *trajectory shape* (not the origin) |

**The recurring move**: the "shared" thing isn't a shared upstream input — it is a **shared downstream channel through which distinct upstream causes flow.** This is structurally important for policy: targeting the destinations / mediators / channels is the leverage, not targeting each upstream cause separately.

## 7. METHODOLOGICAL META-PATTERNS

Three patterns about *how* the program produces refinements:

### 7a. Walk-backs ascend the mechanism
Walk-backs aren't failures; they consistently force a refined framework that's truer than the original.

| original (walked back) | refined (load-bearing) |
|---|---|
| PRE_REG_007 v1 — emigration as cross-country driver signal | v2 emigration as late-stage consequence (VEN chronology + POL aggregate) |
| PRE_REG_018 v1 — 3-type conflict framework | v2 5-type framework (D criminal + E civil-war-mass-displacement emerge from the walk-back) |
| PRE_REG_011 v1 — failed-coup-as-enabler (general) | v2 "failed coup TARGETING democracy" sub-pattern (PER Castillo, USA Jan 6) |
| PRE_REG_036 H — "displacement was the wrong instrument; dense SPEI will show La Niña ≥60%" | rejected; ENSO triangulated-null → reframes the whole search toward **destination convergence** (PRE_REG_040/041) |
| PRE_REG_039 H2 — "drought elevates conflict in coupling cases" | reversed; positive only in SYR/AFG/IRQ → the climate-conflict literature signal is specifically *not* the compound-coupling signal |
| PRE_REG_029 Regime 2b | retired → "boundary not regime" — sharpens the regime definition |

The 32% walk-back rate (≈12/38 fired hypotheses) is **feature, not bug**: every honest walk-back has produced a sharper, truer framework. Several of the program's load-bearing claims (5-type typology, destination-convergence mechanism, emigration-as-consequence) only exist *because* their predecessors were walked back. **Pre-registration discipline is what makes this productive** — without locking predictions in advance, there's no walk-back to learn from.

### 7b. Triangulation across instruments closes the deal
The strongest claims come from cross-instrument convergence; single-instrument findings are provisional.

| paper | claim | instruments converged on |
|---|---|---|
| P8 ENSO-null | single-year ENSO doesn't drive Horn coupling | displacement (034) + box-mean SPEI (036) + area-fraction SPEI (037) — 3 instruments, all ~41-50% La Niña |
| P8 mechanism = destination convergence | coupling lives at destinations | 4 instruments: displacement-coloc (038) + hazard-non-coloc (039) + origin/dest split (040) + burden+temporal (041) |
| P6 typology irreducibility | Stan typology beats baseline | LOO + WDI ablation + Polity ablation — all 3 confirm |
| P4 type validity | 5-type typology is empirical | classifier (018v2 16/18) + DPF (019 5/5) + spatial (020 5/5) + within-country (021 F4 not fired) — 4 supporting properties |
| P1 mechanism | blocking-lever model | success cases (PATTERN_013) + failure cases (PATTERN_027) + sub-indicator recovery (PRE_REG_012) |

**One instrument tells you something; three converging tell you it's real.** This is conservative epistemics built into the substrate.

### 7c. Boundary cases drive refinement
Theoretical progress comes from cases at the boundaries.

| boundary case | refinement it forced |
|---|---|
| ARG/KHM (P2 Regime 2b boundary) | Regime 2b retired → sharpens regime definition |
| USA Trump II (P1 fast-pole 1-in-700) | HYBRID 2x2 cell (between personalist + party-state) |
| TUR unclassified (P4) | bare-threshold IDP boundary; informs typology threshold |
| COD disaster-sparse (P8 Phase 1) | identifies single-channel-dominance limitation → drives Phase 2 (gridded SPEI) |
| BGD+LKA degenerate baseline (P5) | exclusion + method amendment (ratio → recovery-fraction) |
| 2020-22 Horn catastrophe invisible at box-mean (P8 036) | drives PRE_REG_037 area-fraction (resolves dilution alternative) |
| SYR/AFG/IRQ positive drought→conflict (P8 039) | clarifies that classic climate-conflict cases are *not* the compound-coupling cases |
| Hybrid Type-C heterogeneity | sub-typing candidate; drives within-class refinement |

**Boundary cases are where the next pre-reg lives.** A program without good boundary-case attention stagnates; this program's progress is largely driven by attending to them.

## 8. Synthesis — what the meta-patterns imply

### 8a. The recursive structure
The program is recursively doing the same move at multiple scales:

- **Within papers**: find the right analytical unit (paper-level pivot).
- **Across papers (this doc)**: the right *meta*-unit is the destination/mediator/consequence/configuration — not the obvious entity.
- **Across phases of P8 specifically**: each pre-reg pivots one level downstream from the previous (hazards → displacement → origins/destinations → destination-hub × year), and the mechanism emerges only at the deepest pivot.

If this recursive structure is the program's signature, the **policy frame should mirror it**: target the destination hubs (P8), the rent funnel (P7), the discriminating-lever configuration (P1/P5), the regime-period (P2/P4) — not the apparent upstream cause. This is the "intervene at the channel, not the input" framing.

### 8b. What this suggests for an integrative manuscript
The program supports a **methodological / framing paper** beyond the 8 substantive ones. Working title space: *Right-unit-of-analysis in displacement research: a recursive identification framework.* It would argue:

1. The traditional unit of displacement analysis (country, country-year, event) systematically hides the generative mechanism.
2. The correct unit is one resolution finer (sub-national / period-typed / configuration-conditioned).
3. The correct unit is consistently *downstream* of the apparent cause (destination, mediator, consequence).
4. Cross-instrument triangulation + boundary-case attention + walk-back-aware pre-registration produces robust mechanism identification.
5. Policy follows: target the channel/destination, not the input.

This would be a 9th paper that frames the prior 8. (Substrate-level — user's call whether the manuscript writes it.)

### 8c. Live analytical implications
Two implications for the small remaining analytical runway:

- **P2 × P4 twin-paper**: the right unit is the country-period × regime × type cell. The natural test is whether the joint typology (Stan PRE_REG_022, ΔLOO +13.66) predicts outcomes the marginals can't. **Already substantially answered by PRE_REG_022 (typology super-additive +14.22, irreducible to covariates)** — meaning the twin-paper has already been quantitatively closed at the methodology level; what remains is just descriptive presentation of the joint matrix.
- **P8 multi-country generalization**: the limit isn't analytical but data-coverage (IDU single-channel dominance). The destination-convergence mechanism is established for SOM; generalization needs the *destination-hub × year* unit applied to ETH/BRA (currently absent from IDU). The right move is to **construct the destination unit** for ETH/BRA from a different sub-national displacement source (DTM-Ethiopia, OCHA-Brazil) — not to keep mining IDU.

## 9. The single-sentence meta-claim

> **Across all 8 IDP papers, mechanism identification consistently requires re-pivoting the analytical unit one resolution finer than the obvious aggregate and one position downstream of the apparent upstream cause — and the program's distinctive epistemic move is the rigorous, pre-registration-disciplined production of such re-pivots.**

That's the program's signature, in one sentence. Every paper is an instance of it; PRE_REG_038→041 in this last session is the most elaborated demonstration.
