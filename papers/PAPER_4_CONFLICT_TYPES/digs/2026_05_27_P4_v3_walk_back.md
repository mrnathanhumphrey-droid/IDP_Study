## What broke in v3

The Type E narrowing rule `(strife_share + one_sided_share) ≥ 0.20` was the load-bearing change. It was **wrong-directional**.

**Cases over-corrected to UNCLASSIFIED**:

| ISO | v2 | v3 | Why broken |
|---|---|---|---|
| AFG | E | UNCLASSIFIED | Non-state share 3.0% < 20% threshold (but Taliban + ISKP exist as organized opposition) |
| YEM | E | UNCLASSIFIED | Non-state share 2.9% < 20% (but Houthis exist) |
| IRQ | E | UNCLASSIFIED | Non-state share 6.7% < 20% (but IS-Iraq exists) |
| LBY | E | UNCLASSIFIED | Non-state share 4.9% < 20% (but LNA/militias exist) |
| SOM | E | UNCLASSIFIED | Non-state share 6.5% < 20% (Al-Shabaab is the opposition) |
| PHL | E | UNCLASSIFIED | Non-state share 6.0% < 20% (MILF + NPA exist) |

**Root diagnosis**: UCDP fatality-share allocation reflects WHO is dying, not WHO is the organized opposition. In state-vs-rebel civil wars, the state's battle deaths often dominate UCDP because:
- Anti-government rebels die as combatants → some go in side_a state-based fatalities depending on dyad coding
- State-side military casualties are well-documented
- Civilian deaths in clashes don't always classify one-sided

**The 20% non-state share threshold targeted the wrong feature.** "Has organized opposition" is captured in:
- UCDP `conflict_type` column (interstate / intrastate / internationalized intrastate)
- `side_b` actor presence (named non-state armed group)
- NOT in fatality-share by type_of_violence

**Decision**: revert to v2. Document v3 walk-back. File "better organized-opposition feature" as future-work refinement direction.

## A-short rule also failed

AZE 2020-2024 acute window aggregates 2020 (44-day war) + 2023 (one-week offensive) across 5 years. Aggregate stats: 8,262 fat, DPF 407. A-short threshold `DPF ≤ 200` failed.

**Diagnosis**: aggregate-window classification masks short-duration interstate wars. The A-short rule needs **temporal phase decomposition** built in — classify per phase, not per aggregate window. This is the PRE_REG_021 within-country framework applied automatically.
