## What this paper argues (analytical, not prose)

Conflict-displacement structure is determined by **organizational form of violence** (state-army vs predator-militia vs irregular insurgency). Conflicts cluster into **3 distinct types** (with open question of a 4th) based on (a) actor composition, (b) fatality/displacement-per-fatality ratio, and (c) spatial concentration pattern.

This is the **conflict-side parallel** to Paper 2's disaster-displacement typology — twin meta-arguments that corpus heterogeneity decomposes into typology, not noise.
