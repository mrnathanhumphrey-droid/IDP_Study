## Thread 1 — Regime 7 (Wildfire-dominant): CONFIRMED

### Pre-committed rule
- GRC confirmed wildfire-dominant if wildfire-IDP ≥ 60% of total disaster-IDP
- Regime 7 REAL if ≥ 2 additional Mediterranean countries have wildfire share ≥ 30%
- WALKS BACK to GRC-anomaly if GRC is the only one ≥ 30%

### Results (GIDD 2008-2024, wildfire-IDP share of total disaster-IDP)

| Country | Total IDP | Wildfire share | Top channel | Verdict |
|---|---:|---:|---|:---:|
| CYP | 1,710 | **99.2%** | Wildfire | member |
| GRC | 290,731 | **82.1%** | Wildfire | member (anchor) |
| PRT | 15,968 | **80.3%** | Wildfire | member |
| ESP | 199,358 | **71.2%** | Wildfire | member |

- GRC wildfire 82.1% ≥ 60% → confirmed dominant ✓
- Co-validators ≥30%: ESP (71.2), PRT (80.3), CYP (99.2), FRA (61.1) — **4 members, all ≥60%** → threshold of ≥2 cleared decisively
- **Regime 7 REAL. F1 NOT FIRED.**

### Clean mechanism discrimination
The test correctly separates the Mediterranean basin into two distinct displacement regimes:
- **Regime 7 (Wildfire)**: GRC, ESP, PRT, CYP, FRA — Mediterranean fire-climate (hot dry summers + fire-prone vegetation + wind-driven spread)
- **Regime 6 (Earthquake)**: ITA, TUR, HRV — Mediterranean seismicity (Apennine, Anatolian, Dinaric tectonics)

This is a strong result: two countries can be geographic neighbors in the same basin yet belong to different displacement regimes because the load-bearing physical mechanism differs. It reinforces Paper 2's core claim that **physical geography (not income/governance) determines displacement structure** — and here the geography is fire-climate vs tectonics, both Mediterranean.

### GIDD taxonomy note
The earlier PATTERN_029 framing ("82.1% Other channel") was an artifact of bucketing only 5 main channels (Flood/Storm/EQ/Drought/Conflict). The full GIDD hazard taxonomy has **Wildfire as a top-level Hazard Type** (2,168 events globally), so wildfire dominance is directly confirmable, not inferred from a residual.

### FRA caveat
France at 61.1% is the weakest member (others ≥71%). FRA has substantial flood + storm exposure too. It clears the wildfire-top-channel bar but is the boundary member of Regime 7; worth flagging if regime membership is reported with confidence tiers.

## Thread 2 — Regime 2b (Flood-dominant transitional): WALKED BACK

### Pre-committed rule
- ARG/KHM confirmed transitional if both have flood ≥ 70% AND storm in [10%, 30%]
- Regime 2b REAL if ≥ 2 additional candidates (COL/IRQ/VNM/BGD) also fall in the gap
- WALKS BACK to boundary-documentation if only ARG/KHM qualify

### Results

| Country | Flood | Storm | flood max/median | In gap? | Actual regime |
|---|---:|---:|---:|:---:|---|
| ARG | 85.4% | 11.1% | 5.0× | ✓ | boundary |
| KHM | 75.7% | 23.8% | 10.6× | ✓ | boundary |
| COL | 92.9% | 2.9% | 82.8× | ✗ | storm <10% → R2-like |
| THA | 94.3% | 5.7% | 130× | ✗ | R1 bimodal-mega-flood |
| PAK | 95.0% | 0.6% | 65.1× | ✗ | R1 (reference) |
| IRQ | 44.8% | 0.3% | 5.9× | ✗ | not flood-dominant |
| VNM | 10.8% | 89.0% | 15.9× | ✗ | storm-dominant R3 |
| BGD | 36.1% | 63.5% | 4.8× | ✗ | storm-leaning R4b |

- ARG + KHM both qualify ✓ (anchor candidates)
- Additional candidates in gap: **none** → **< 2 threshold; F2 FIRES**
- **Regime 2b WALKS BACK to boundary-documentation.**

### Why it walked back
The candidates that are flood-dominant either have storm <10% (COL — closer to a steady-flood R2 profile) or are extreme-bimodal R1 (THA, PAK with max/median 65-130×). The flood≥70% + storm 10-30% window is genuinely sparse — only ARG (La Plata basin + Atlantic storm exposure) and KHM (Mekong + secondary cyclone) sit there. Two countries do not make a regime; they make a documented transition zone between R1 and R4.

This is an honest walk-back on a pre-committed falsifier — the kind of result that distinguishes a typology built on evidence from one built on aesthetic completeness. Not every gap is a regime.
