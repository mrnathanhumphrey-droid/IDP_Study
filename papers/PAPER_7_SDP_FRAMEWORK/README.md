## What this paper argues (analytical, not prose)

Out of respect for the IDP term's protections, we coin **SDP (Structurally Displaced Persons)** as a parallel analytical concept. Whether SDP and IDP describe the same mechanism is treated as an empirical question. The channel-orthogonality + residue-class methodology (Papers 2 + 4 + 6) transfers from IDP to SDP.

US homelessness is the anchor case. Force-criterion: state revealed-preference for war-making over welfare-making spending, operating at two scales (first-order total allocation; second-order within-welfare allocation priorities).

**Locked**: framework is correlational + revealed-preference, never causal.
