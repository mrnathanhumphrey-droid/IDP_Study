**Established mechanism (the Paper 8 headline):**
> Compound-crisis coupling is **contemporaneous displacement-destination convergence**. Two spatially-distinct, independently-driven hazards — drought in pastoral peripheries, armed conflict in contested zones — displace populations from distinct origins to a small set of shared receiving hubs that absorb the majority of displacement and take in both flows in the same years. The national year-to-year coupling (PRE_REG_033) is the aggregate signature of these few destination hubs. The compound crisis is realized at the destinations, not where the hazards strike.

**Policy corollary (quantified):** the compound burden falls on a handful of receiving areas (SOM: 8 grid cells = 56% of national displacement) that experience neither hazard directly. Target compound-crisis response at destination hubs, not hazard zones.

**Optional remaining**: name the 8 SOM hubs (Mogadishu / Baidoa / regional capitals — point-in-polygon vs GADM); multi-country generalization bounded by IDU single-channel dominance (could augment with sub-national displacement sources for ETH/BRA).
