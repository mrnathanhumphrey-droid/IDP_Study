## 1. Mechanism claim

Democratic backsliding is **neither inevitable nor irreversible**. Two empirically tractable structures govern resilience:

1. **Blocking levers** (PATTERN_027): a small set of structural features determine whether an executive-aggrandizement attempt consolidates or fails. When ≥3 of four levers are present, backsliding is blocked; when ≤2, it consolidates.

2. **Mirror-order recovery** (PRE_REG_012): when institutions DO recover from a backsliding episode, the order of recovery is the reverse of the order of capture. Vertical-accountability institutions (elections, opposition autonomy) — captured last — recover last and slowest. Horizontal institutions (courts, legislative oversight) — captured first — recover first and fastest.

Together these make democratic resilience **predictable**: you can score a country's blocking levers to forecast whether an attempt will consolidate, and you can forecast the *sequence and tempo* of recovery once restoration begins.

## 2. The four blocking levers (PATTERN_027, already firmed)

From 5 blocked cases (BRA Bolsonaro, ISR judicial overhaul, USA Trump I, KOR Yoon martial law, PER Castillo self-coup) vs 7 consolidated cases (SLV, HUN, TUR, VEN, POL-PiS, TUN, BLR):

| Lever | Mechanism | Blocked cases that had it |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Independent Constitutional/Supreme Court** | court refuses to ratify captures; can strike legislation | BRA STF, USA SCOTUS (2020), KOR Constitutional Court, ISR Supreme Court |
| **2. Mass civil-society mobilization capacity** | sustained street + institutional opposition | ISR protests, KOR protests, BRA Jan-8 backlash |
| **3. Electoral cycle within reach** | natural reset mechanism if election lands mid-attempt | USA 2020, BRA 2022 |
| **4. Federal/regional parallel power** | distributes resistance points | USA states, BRA states |

**Decision rule (pre-committed)**: ≥3 levers present → blocked; ≤2 → consolidates. The one within-corpus complication is PER (Castillo's self-coup blocked in hours, but successor Boluarte consolidated via *legal* channels) — showing that blocking a dramatic self-coup ≠ blocking incremental legalistic backsliding.

## 3. Mirror-order recovery (PRE_REG_012, already STRONGLY SUPPORTED 6/6)

When recovery happens, sub-indicators recover in the REVERSE of capture order:

| Tier | Sub-indicators | Median recovery fraction (in-sample) |
|---|---|---|
| FAST (recover first) | judicial constraints (122%), media (91%), horizontal accountability (90%), free expression (87%), high-court independence (84%), diagonal accountability (80%) | ≥ 80% |
| SLOW (recover last) | vertical accountability (9%), elections free+fair (7%), opposition autonomy (2%) | ≤ 10% |

Confirmed in 6/6 testable recovery-from-backsliding cases (POL, BRA, KOR, BGD, Sri Lanka, ZMB). **Refinement**: applies to recoveries FROM active backsliding, NOT transitions FROM long-standing authoritarianism (KOR-1987, IDN-1998 operate differently — wholesale regime replacement, no captured architecture to outlast the leader).

## 5. Novel contributions (proposed; 4)

1. **Four-lever blocking model** with a pre-committed decision rule (≥3 blocks) — predictive, not just descriptive
2. **Mirror-order recovery** — recovery reverses capture; vertical recovers last (6/6 confirmed)
3. **Recovery velocity asymmetry** — horizontal institutions recover in months, vertical in years (or never within window)
4. **The lever-erosion warning** — USA 2020→2025 shows blocking levers can DEGRADE between attempts (captured SCOTUS + party-state fusion), converting a "blocked" country into an "at-risk" one. Resilience is not permanent.

## 7. Falsification posture

The paper's central claims are already substantially supported (PATTERN_027 4-lever + PRE_REG_012 6/6). The NEW pre-regs (030-032) carry the falsification stakes:
- If the lever-score does NOT separate blocked from consolidated out-of-sample → blocking model is post-hoc storytelling
- If recovery velocity is uniform across tiers → mirror-order is an artifact of measurement, not sequence
- If recoveries uniformly return to baseline → no "ceiling" finding; recovery is complete and the vertical-lag is temporary not structural
